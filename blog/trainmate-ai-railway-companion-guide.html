2. Coach & Berth Layout Guidance: Never Guess Your Seat Type Again
One of the most frequent dilemmas for train passengers is understanding their assigned seat layout before stepping inside the coach. Whether you are traveling in 3-Tier AC (3A), 2-Tier AC (2A), 3AC Economy (3E), or Sleeper Class (SL), TrainMate AI provides instant mathematical blueprint breakdown:
•
Berth Type Identification: Instantly know if your berth is Lower, Middle, Upper, Side Lower, or Side Upper based on Indian Railways standard 8-berth and 6-berth bay calculations.
•
Charging Socket Locations: Find out where power outlets are located across compartments and side berths for keeping your laptop and phone charged.
•
Middle Berth Etiquette: Understand official sleeping hours (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) and passenger rights regarding middle berth folding.
•
Coach Amenities: Learn about linen/bedroll provisions, onboard reading lights, and AC temperature control policies.
💡 Smart Berth Tip
If you have a Side Lower (SL) berth, remember that during daytime hours (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM), the Side Upper passenger has the right to sit on the lower seat with you!

4. Emergency Helpline 139 & Railway Safety Support
Safety and emergency response are paramount during long-distance train journeys. TrainMate AI provides rapid guidance on railway safety protocols and official grievance redressal:
•
Helpline 139: Integrated single emergency helpline for security, medical emergencies, catering complaints, and child assistance.
•
RailMadad Grievance System: Step-by-step guidance on raising live tickets for berth cleanliness, AC malfunctioning, or unauthorized passengers.
•
RPF Assistance: Knowing how to contact onboard Railway Protection Force (RPF) and Government Railway Police (GRP) escorts during night journeys.

Luggage & Prohibited Items: Official allowances and safety guidelines for carrying luggage and electronics securely.

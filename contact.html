Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about getting help with TrainMate
How do I report an issue with the app?
Email us at support@trainmate.in with your registered mobile number and a description of the issue. Include screenshots if possible.
Can I suggest a new feature?
Absolutely! We love hearing from our users. Send your feature suggestions to support@trainmate.in or use the in-app feedback form.
Is TrainMate available on iOS?
Currently, TrainMate is available on Android via Google Play Store and as a web app. iOS support is planned for a future release.
How do I delete my account?
You can request account deletion by emailing support@trainmate.in from your registered email. We will process your request within 48 hours.

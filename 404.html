What is TrainParcel?
TrainParcel is a peer-to-peer train parcel delivery network that connects senders needing urgent parcel shipping with verified train travelers who have extra luggage carrying capacity. Senders save time and money with same-day station handoffs, while travelers earn extra income by carrying parcels along their existing train routes.
